What is Steganography?

🔒

Hide in Plain Sight

Steganography is the art of hiding information within ordinary-looking files. Unlike encryption, which scrambles data, steganography conceals the very existence of the message.

🎯

LSB Technique

We use the Least Significant Bit method, modifying the last bit of each pixel's RGB values. Changes are invisible to the human eye but can store substantial text data.

🛡️

100% Private

Everything happens in your browser. No files are uploaded to servers, no data leaves your device. Complete privacy and security for your secret messages.

📊

High Capacity

A single 720p image can store approximately 345KB of text - that's about 70,000 words! Perfect for hiding documents, notes, or long messages.
